# MailWhere v0.11.0 — Local mail mirror and FTS5 search

## What changed
- Added a read-only local mirror of available Outlook Inbox and Sent Items mail.
- Added normalized plain-text body storage and SQLite FTS5 search in `followups.sqlite`.
- Added resumable full backfill, incremental synchronization, and completed-inventory reconciliation.
- Added `search-mail --json` for SQLite-only search without opening Outlook.
- Added sanitized mirror diagnostics, explicit FTS rebuild support, and stale-source handling.

## Safety and performance
- Outlook access remains read-only.
- `(StoreID, EntryID)` is treated as a current locator rather than stable identity.
- Incomplete or warning-bearing inventory scans never delete unseen mirror rows.
- Writes are serialized in batches of at most 25 rows; direct oversized batches are rejected.
- The native SQLite bundle is pinned to `2.1.12` to exclude the vulnerable `2.1.11` dependency.
- Attachments, embeddings, and contextWhere full-body copies remain out of scope.
